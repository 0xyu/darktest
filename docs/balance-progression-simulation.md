## Reading the numbers

- The strategy is a reproducible ACCEPTANCE PROXY, not the player's AUTO logic and
  not a claim that every build performs the same.
- `c` is frozen at stage build, each surviving enemy retaliates once per player action
  (M3 semantics), and the hero's HP is carried between stages, so the replay loop after
  a defeat is the real recovery mechanism rather than an extra bonus.
- Attack actions and movement turns are reported separately, so a run cannot hide a
  damage shortfall behind standing still.
- Level tracking is measured at BATTLE START (§5), not after the stage's own kills.
